Taker flow splits each trade by the side that crossed the spread: a taker buy lifts the offer, a taker sell hits the bid. Net taker flow is taker buys minus taker sells in notional dollars (underlying exposure traded, not margin). A taker buy can open a long or close a short, so read it with the change in open interest.
